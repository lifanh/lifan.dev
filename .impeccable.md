## Design Context

### Users
Lifan Dev serves a dual audience: **Lifan himself** (as a personal brand and developer portfolio) and **other developers** exploring interactive educational tools for economics, accounting, and philosophy. Users arrive with intellectual curiosity—they're developers who want to understand new domains through hands-on simulations, not passive reading. The context is self-directed learning: unhurried, focused, and exploratory.

### Brand Personality
**Refined, Curious, Purposeful.**

- **Voice:** Clear and confident without being cold. Explains complex ideas simply. Respects the reader's intelligence.
- **Tone:** Thoughtful and precise—every word earns its place. Warm enough to invite exploration, restrained enough to feel credible.
- **Emotional goal:** Evoke **curiosity and discovery**—a sense that there's something interesting to explore, and the interface won't get in your way.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone:** Minimalist elegance through sophisticated restraint. Inspired by **Apple's restraint**—generous whitespace, limited palette, precision in every detail.
- **Reference:** Apple.com — confidence through simplicity, letting content breathe, interaction as the focal point.
- **Anti-reference:** Not a typical Bootstrap site — no generic card grids, no gratuitous color splashes, no cookie-cutter layout patterns. Every element should feel intentional and handcrafted.
- **Theme:** Full light and dark mode support. Slate neutral foundation with a single blue accent reserved exclusively for interaction. No pure black or pure white.
- **Character:** The design should feel like a well-curated workshop — everything has its place, nothing is decorative without purpose, and the craft is evident in the details.

### Design Principles
1. **Restraint is sophistication.** Use minimal colors, limited font weights (400/500/700 only), and generous whitespace. When in doubt, remove rather than add.
2. **Accent means action.** Blue is exclusively for interactive elements—buttons, links, focus rings. If it's blue, it's clickable. No exceptions.
3. **Space creates focus.** White space is not empty—it's a design element that directs attention and creates visual rhythm. Embrace it.
4. **Accessibility is non-negotiable.** WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1), visible focus rings, 44×44px touch targets, respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`. Inclusive by default.
5. **Consistency compounds.** Use the 4px spacing scale, the design token system, and the component patterns religiously. Small inconsistencies erode trust; precision builds it.
